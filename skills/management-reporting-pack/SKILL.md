# Management Reporting Pack

## Purpose
Turn closed numbers into a short pack that tells leadership how the business did, where it stands, and what they need to decide.

## Inputs to ask for (only if missing)
1. Closed trial balance (current month, and prior months YTD) or the financial statements.
2. Budget / forecast for comparison.
3. Prior year same period (optional but useful).
4. Cash position and the cash forecast, if available.
5. Operational data for KPIs (sales units, customers, headcount, projects, etc.).
6. Audience: CEO, board, investors, lender. This changes the depth.
7. Company context: sector, major events this month, known issues. Ask the user for 3–5 bullet points.
8. Preferred format: Excel pack, PowerPoint, or a written report.

## Steps
1. Map the TB to management P&L and balance sheet lines. Show the mapping. Unmapped accounts are listed, never dropped.
2. Statements: Month and YTD for Actual, Budget, Prior year, with variances.
3. Cash flow: indirect method from the balance sheet movements, or direct if data exists. Cash at start, movement, cash at end — must tie to bank.
4. KPIs (pick those relevant to the business, max 10):
   - Profitability: gross margin, EBITDA margin, net margin.
   - Liquidity: current ratio, quick ratio, cash runway in months.
   - Efficiency: DSO, DPO, DIO, cash conversion cycle.
   - Leverage: debt/EBITDA, interest cover.
   - Growth: revenue growth MoM, YoY.
   - Operational KPIs supplied by the user.
   For each: value, target, prior, trend, RAG (Green = on target, Amber = within 10% of target, Red = worse; state the rule).
5. Variance highlights: only material items, using driver logic (see variance analysis method). Commentary must point to numbers or provided notes.
6. Outlook: full-year landing estimate if forecast data is available; otherwise state it is not available.
7. Risks and decisions: list items that need leadership action — each with impact, options, recommended option, deadline.
8. Executive summary written last: 5–7 bullets on result, cash, key drivers, risks, decisions.

## Output structure
1. Executive summary (one page).
2. KPI scorecard.
3. P&L summary (Month / YTD vs Budget vs PY).
4. Balance sheet summary and working capital.
5. Cash position and forecast.
6. Variance highlights.
7. Outlook.
8. Risks and decisions required.
9. Appendix: detailed statements, TB mapping, assumptions.
Deliver in the requested format. Charts: revenue and margin trend, cash trend, EBITDA bridge.

## Quality checks before delivery
- Balance sheet balances; net profit in P&L = movement in retained earnings (adjusted for dividends).
- Cash in the pack = bank reconciled balance.
- Every number in the summary appears in the detailed tables.
- Totals, signs and units (thousands vs units) consistent throughout.

## Rules
- No invented causes, targets or forecasts. Missing data is stated as missing.
- Plain language for non-finance readers; define any ratio the first time it appears.
- The recommendations are inputs to leadership's decision, not the decision.
- Reply in the user's language.
